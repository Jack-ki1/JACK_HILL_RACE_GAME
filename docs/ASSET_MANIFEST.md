# Procedural Asset & Audio Manifest (`jack_hill_game`)

All 3D models, PBR textures, normal/bump maps, SVG icons, and sound effects are procedurally generated in code (zero external runtime binary asset downloads).

- **3D Vehicles, Leonida Customizations & Trail Accessories (`src/game/models.ts`)**:
  - **Shared Cabin, Chassis & Running Gear**: Crisp body shutlines, door handles, bolted fender flares with silver rivets (`ranger`/`monster`), bucket seats with headrests, dashboard with illuminated gauge pod, center shifter lever, steering column/wheel, side mirrors, welded steel stinger bumper with fog lamps & D-ring shackles, expedition roof rack carrying traction recovery board + 4 X-taped round rally floodlights (`(X)(X)(X)(X)`), radiator core backing mesh behind grilles, dual exhaust pipe bore tips with upshift & lift-off backfire flame cones (`backfireGlow`), reactive brake-light emissive materials, Halo LED DRL headlight rings, Leonida `JACK-06` sunset license plates, clearer glass glazing (`opacity: 0.34`), and weathered paint micro-grain (`createWeatheredPaintBumpTexture`).
  - **7-Coil High-Contrast Suspension & Mud-Terrain Wheels**: Deep-dish stepped barrel rims + recessed inner barrels + 12 lip bolts + 5 center lug nuts, 20 staggered mud-terrain chevron tread blocks + outer side-biter teeth, perforated brake disc rotors, painted brake calipers, articulated `armRear` / `armFront` control arms, and 7-coil helical coilover shock assemblies (`buildCoiloverShock`).
  - **Dynamic Dirt / Grime Overlay (`dirtOverlayMesh` / `dirtMat`)**: Accumulates dynamically with distance driven, wheelspin, and landing impacts across each stage, tinted per biome.
  - **Selectable Neon Underglow (`NEON_COLORS`)**: 6 chassis underglow options (`off`, `vice_pink`, `leonida_cyan`, `miami_mint`, `sunset_amber`, `uv_violet`) with physical chassis light tubes + additive ground neon pool (`neonPoolMesh`) and showroom turntable glow ring tinting.
  - **Selectable Liveries (`LIVERY_DEFS`)**: 7 body graphic packages (`clean`, `vice_stripes`, `carbon_hood`, `leonida_interceptor`, `sunset_rally`, `sticker_bomb`, `viper_stripe`).
  - **Selectable 3D Accessories**: `lightbar`, `bullbar`, `whip_flag`, and `snorkel` (`Ram-Air Snorkel`: +4% torque & +3% top speed).
  - **Ghost Rival Silhouette (`buildGhostVehicle3D`)**: Subtle warm amber-gold silhouette (`opacity: 0.28`, `depthWrite: false`) rendered at `z = -1.35` strictly during `PLAYING` mode when enabled.
  - **Articulated Human Driver**: Natural sculpted human driver (textured hair, jaw, brow, eyes, ears, charcoal trail shirt, skin forearms gripping the steering wheel) on all 4-wheelers (`ranger`, `monster`, `buggy`, `muscle`, `cheetah_vc`, `donk_leonida`), plus sleek racing helmet & visor on `superbike`.
  - **7 Vehicles**: `ranger`, `monster`, `buggy`, `muscle`, `superbike`, `cheetah_vc`, and `donk_leonida`.
- **Procedural Textures & Bump Maps (`src/game/textures.ts`)**:
  - `createCoinTexture`, `createTerrainStrataTexture` (fractured shelves & stone flecks), `createRoadTrackTexture` (512×512 rock slabs/gravel + Vice Coastway asphalt markings), `createLicensePlateTexture`, `createSurfaceBumpTexture` (512×512 multi-octave rock fracture `NoColorSpace` bump map), `createTireBumpTexture` (512×256 chevron mud lugs `NoColorSpace`), `createWeatheredPaintBumpTexture` (`NoColorSpace`), `createRallyLightCoverTexture` (matte black housing + crisp white `X` tape), `createDirtGrimeTexture`, `createWoodPlankTexture`, `createGarageFloorTexture`, `createEarthTexture`, `createSignpostTexture`, and `createSoftGlowTexture`.
- **Custom SVG Vector Iconography (`src/components/GameIcons.tsx`, `src/components/WorldMapModal.tsx`)**:
  - Coin, Gem, Fuel Canister, Driver Avatar, Rank Shield (`RankShieldIcon`), 7 Vehicle Silhouettes (`VehicleSilhouetteIcon`), Upgrade Part icons (`UpgradePartIcon`), Bronze Crate, Gold Chest, Nitro Bottle, Coin Magnet, plus custom procedural biome hero SVGs inside Stage Select.
- **5 Biomes, Craggy Rock Terrain & Dual-Angle Perspective World (`src/game/worldScene.ts`, `src/game/garageScene.ts`)**:
  - Biomes: `vice_coast` (Vice Coastway), `countryside`, `red_canyon`, `frost_peaks`, and `moon`.
  - Dual-angle `THREE.PerspectiveCamera`: unobstructed low front-right 3/4 rock-crawling hero showcase on `TITLE`, transitioning smoothly to a crisp 2.5D side-follow chase camera (`fov = 38`) during `PLAYING`.
  - 5-octave FBM volumetric cloud deck shader with golden-hour Rayleigh solar break and subtle contrails, faceted vertex-perturbed craggy rock slabs (`createCraggyRockMesh`), dry scrub grass tufts (`createScrubGrassTuft`), curved Leonida Palm Trees, Vice Neon Highway Sign Gantries, Lifeguard Towers, Cave Tunnels, wooden/causeway bridges, and terrain-tracking contact shadows (water skips removed).
  - Workshop garage bay defaulting to `profile` camera matching gameplay framing, with 6 presets (`profile`, `hero`, `front`, `rear`, `cockpit`, `wheels`), turntable radial contact shadow plane, high-contrast 3-point key/warm/cool rim lighting, and gantry/lift props.
- **Web Audio (`src/game/audio.ts`)**:
  - Vehicle-pitched dual-oscillator engine synth tuned per all 7 vehicles, bandpass noise Nitro jet, upshift exhaust pop (`playGearShift()`), coins, fuel arpeggio, stunt fanfare, suspension landing thud, crash sweep, UI clicks, and Master & SFX volume controls.
